Animals Memory

Privacy Policy

Application: Animals Memory (Android) Effective Date: October 20, 2026
Summary: Animals Memory is built with user privacy as a core principle. The app operates completely offline. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or device information. There are no ads, no trackers, and no background services.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Animals Memory ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe, ad-free gaming experience for users of all ages.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Animals Memory does not collect any personal data.

The game functions entirely offline on your mobile device. Specifically:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to play the game.
  • Local Gameplay Data Only: Player nicknames entered for high scores, game scores, and sound/music toggle preferences are stored exclusively on your device using Android's local storage (SharedPreferences).
  • No Server Transmission: The game does not transmit high scores, nicknames, gameplay statistics, or any other information to any external server or third party.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The app does not integrate any third-party software development kits (SDKs) that gather data. Specifically:

  • No Advertisements: Animals Memory contains zero advertisements (AdMob and all third-party advertising SDKs are completely excluded).
  • No Analytics: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, or any analytics tools to track your in-game behavior or device parameters.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Because no data is collected, no data is ever shared with, sold to, or accessible by third parties.

4. Device Permissions

Animals Memory requires no sensitive permissions to operate:

  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • No Location access (neither precise nor coarse).
  • No Contacts, Phone, or SMS access.
  • No External Storage reading or writing.
  • No Internet access required (network permissions are omitted).

5. Children's Privacy & Family Policy Compliance

Animals Memory is designed for players of all ages, including children and families:

  • The game complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).
  • Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, parents and guardians can rest assured that children's privacy is fully respected.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

All game data (such as high scores and sound settings) resides strictly in your device's local application storage. You have full control over this data:

  • You can clear all saved scores and settings at any time by going to your device's Settings > Apps > Animals Memory > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the application automatically deletes all locally stored data.

7. Security

Because Animals Memory does not collect or transmit information over the internet, your gameplay data cannot be intercepted, leaked, or accessed remotely. Local data security relies on the standard security sandbox provided by the Android Operating System.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this URL with an updated "Effective Date". We encourage you to review this page periodically.
